    I am a developer and researcher with a strong multidisciplinary background, holding a Master’s degree in Multimedia Engineering from the Faculty of Engineering of the University of Porto (FEUP) and a Bachelor’s degree in Physics Engineering from the University of Minho. My academic path has given me a solid foundation in mathematics, physics, and computer science, which I apply to real-world problem-solving through technology. My main interests include machine learning, deep learning, and neural networks, as well as web development, software engineering, DevOps, robotics and automation, and financial technology. I am also passionate about scientific modeling and simulation, and I enjoy building systems that combine rigorous technical knowledge with creativity and innovation. As a developer, I have experience across the software stack, from backend and database design to frontend development and user experience. I also explore the integration of artificial intelligence into interactive and immersive systems, always with the goal of creating solutions that are impactful, accessible, and scalable. I am particularly motivated by the opportunity to work on projects at the intersection of technology and society, where emerging tools like AI, automation, and immersive computing can improve lives and foster innovation.
